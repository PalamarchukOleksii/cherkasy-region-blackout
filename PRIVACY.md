# Privacy Policy — Cherkasy Region Blackout

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Cherkasy Region Blackout is a home-screen widget that shows the hourly power outage schedule (ГПВ) published by
Cherkasyoblenergo.

**No personal data is collected.** The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reporting, and it does not
use location, contacts or any other personal information.

**Network access.** The app downloads public schedule pages directly from `www.cherkasyoblenergo.com`. Those
requests go only to that site, and like any web request they show your IP address to it. Nothing else is sent
anywhere.

**Stored on your device.** The last downloaded schedule and the queue (черга) you chose stay in the app's private
storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

The app is not affiliated with Cherkasyoblenergo.

Questions: open an issue in this GitHub repository.
